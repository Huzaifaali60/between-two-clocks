---
title: "What if my art lived on a paper cup?"
date: 2026-11-04
mood: inspired
draft: true
---

I keep thinking about how many paper cups get thrown away in Lahore every day. Thousands. Each one is a tiny canvas that nobody looks at twice.

So here is the idea. My drawings, on cups, in cafés. People hold them for ten minutes and maybe take a photo. That is ten minutes of someone looking at something I made.

I made a list of five cafés tonight. I'm nervous about walking in. However, I've been nervous about everything worth doing, so that's probably a good sign.
