title: "Where would a vampire live in Lahore?"
date: 2026-10-15
mood: curious
draft: true
---

Not in a castle. Lahore doesn't do castles. A vampire here would live somewhere old, crowded and loud, where nobody notices one more person awake at night.

I walked through the old city this evening thinking about Lahore After Dark. Every balcony looked like a hiding place. Every narrow street looked like a chapter.

I came home and wrote 1,200 words. Then I deleted 400 of them. Still, 800 is more than zero.
