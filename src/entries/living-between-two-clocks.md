My phone shows two times. One for where I am and one for where my work is. After three years, I still glance at both before I answer anyone.

Some days it feels like a superpower. I get quiet mornings and quiet nights. Other days I feel like I belong to neither city.

Today was the second kind. So I'm writing this instead of scrolling. That counts as a small win.
