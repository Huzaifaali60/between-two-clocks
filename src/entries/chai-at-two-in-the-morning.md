The London shift ended at two. Lahore was asleep, but the kettle wasn't. I stood by the window and listened to a motorbike go somewhere it probably shouldn't.

This is the hour I like best. Nobody needs anything from me. The emails have stopped. The city is quiet enough that I can hear my own thoughts, and tonight they were mostly about chapter nine.

I think Imtisal needs to lose something before she wins anything. I wrote that on a sticky note and stuck it to the laptop. Tomorrow I'll find out if I meant it.
